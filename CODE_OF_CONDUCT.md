# Code of Conduct

DesktopElegans welcomes technical discussion, bug reports, biological modelling ideas and focused contributions.

## Expected behaviour

Be respectful, specific and constructive.

When reviewing code or discussing a bug:

- criticise the implementation, not the person;
- provide reproducible evidence where possible;
- distinguish observed behaviour from assumptions;
- accept that Windows-specific bugs may require hardware or monitor-layout details;
- keep disagreements technical and proportionate.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory behaviour, deliberate disruption and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Project boundary

DesktopElegans is a local desktop-organism simulation. Requests or contributions whose primary purpose is malware behaviour are outside the project's scope and may be closed without implementation.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, remove or close content that violates these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the project usable and constructive.
